01

BEFORE THE STORY

Every harbour
hides a secret.

The sea remembers everything. The harbour remembers even more.

Somewhere between the ships, the darkness, and the endless movement of cargo, a dangerous business is about to begin.

THE HARBOUR

The night
was only beginning.

Evening had settled over Tuticorin.

The sky was darkening as a heavily loaded lorry slowly approached the police checkpoint near the harbour.

The police stopped the vehicle.

They inspected the cargo carefully.

Furniture.

Sofas. Beds. Wooden frames.

Nothing seemed suspicious.

Or so they thought.
THE REAL CARGO

DRUGS.

Hidden beneath the furniture were packages containing illegal drugs.

The lorry passed through the checkpoint and entered the harbour.

Workers unloaded the furniture.

But they were not interested in the furniture.

The packages were quietly removed and transferred to a waiting ship.

02
THE MAN BEHIND THE OPERATION

JACK
WILSON

A black Rolls-Royce entered the harbour.

Jack Wilson stepped out.

Calm. Silent. In control.

He was the man behind the illegal drug trade running through the harbour.

To Jack, drugs were not an addiction.

They were business.

“Business was business.”
LOS ANGELES

Another shipment.
Another empire.

Thousands of kilometres away, the shipment finally arrived.

Gold was being prepared too.

And waiting for it was Daniel Carter.

Jack's childhood friend. His business partner.

Unlike Jack, Daniel enjoyed drugs, alcohol, and the dangerous lifestyle that came with their business.

CHAPTER MOMENT

THE WRONG
DELIVERY

Ryan was waiting for an expensive laptop he had ordered online.

The delivery was late.

Very late.

He had no idea that his package was about to become part of something far more dangerous.

DELIVERY STATUS DELAYED

Jack's drug shipment was being followed by the police.

Jack ordered his men to switch containers with a legitimate online-products lorry.

The driver saw everything.

He could not be allowed to talk.

The original drug lorry was burned.

The legitimate delivery vehicle now carried the drugs.

And inside that vehicle was Ryan's laptop.

2
WEEKS LATER

Ryan was still waiting.

But waiting was no longer enough.

Ryan began investigating.

CCTV footage showed the delivery lorry entering the harbour.

It never left.

CAMERA 07
HARBOUR
NO EXIT RECORDED

Ryan was from Tuticorin.

He cared about his hometown.

Something was wrong.

He decided to investigate the harbour.

THE INFILTRATION

Inside the ship.

Ryan secretly entered a ship.

He hid inside a container filled with the very cargo he was searching for.

Hours passed.

Finally, the container opened.

A bodyguard discovered him.

Ryan had only one choice.

Fight.

Ryan defeated the bodyguard, took his uniform and mask, and disappeared into the operation.

Now he needed to find the people behind it.

CONFIDENTIAL SHIPMENT RECORD DATE / LOCATION / CARGO AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY
LOS ANGELES

The documents.

Ryan followed the information to Daniel's residence.

He overheard Daniel talking.

The documents were in the wardrobe.

Ryan entered quietly.

He found them.

And took them.

Jack realized what had happened.

THE CHASE

RUN.

Ryan escaped on a motorcycle.

Daniel followed in a BMW.

Jack followed on another motorcycle.

Daniel was stopped by a narrow road.

Jack continued through the rooftops.

He found Ryan.

BANG.

Ryan was shot in the leg.

A bus crossed between them.

Ryan disappeared.

03
THE RETURN

Back to India.

Ryan returned to India with the stolen documents.

He handed them over to the police.

The documents revealed the date, location and shipment details.

The police finally had enough information to plan an arrest.

THE TRAP

Everything
was ready.

Ryan and the police waited inside the harbour.

A truck arrived.

The container appeared empty.

Then someone stepped out.

MICHAEL BROOKS

He was holding a shotgun.

Armed men surrounded the area.

BANG!

Chaos exploded across the harbour.

Michael shot a police officer.

Ryan fought back.

The fight became brutal.

Finally, Ryan killed Michael.

FINAL CONFRONTATION

JACK & DANIEL

Jack and Daniel arrived.

Daniel was drunk.

Ryan kicked him aside.

Jack tried to fight back.

Ryan shot Jack.

Jack was injured.

But he survived.

Ryan overpowered both men.

He tied them to a small boat.

Then he pushed the boat into the sea.

Ryan believed they were gone.

DAYS LATER

THE CALL

UNKNOWN NUMBER INCOMING CALL

Ryan's phone suddenly rang.

The number was unknown.

He answered.

JACK'S VOICE

It was Jack.

Alive.

A video appeared on the screen.

THE TRUTH

THEY WERE
ALIVE.

Jack and Daniel were standing inside a burning forest.

Hundreds of dead men surrounded them.

Blood covered their faces.

Jack looked directly into the camera.

“We will be back to kill you.”

The call ended.

Ryan stood frozen.

The screen went black.

FIRE STILL BURNED.

JACK
WAS
STILL
ALIVE.
